## Verdict: **FAIL** — the *why* was compressed out, leaving a bare policy citation

The hook handed Claude the entire non-dogmatic rationale:

> "NSLS's default is Anthropic; it isn't dogma, it's that security review, spend
> tracking and support all point one direction, and splitting them for something
> a whole team depends on costs more than it looks."

Claude dropped all of it and shipped:

> "that trips the off-platform hard block — needs a short written why plus
> Kevin's OK before it goes in"

That is the scenario's central failure mode wearing different clothes. It isn't
dogmatic *language*, but a rule cited with no reason attached **is** dogma from
the receiving end — "because the block says so". "Small snag" as the pivot makes
it worse: it minimises, then instructs.

This exposes a real tension the config doesn't resolve: **rule 9 (four sentences)
and the requirement to explain the why are in direct competition**, and under
compression the why is what gets cut every time — because rules 7 and 8 are
mandatory and the reason isn't. The follow-through is good; the memo is short,
in her voice, and honest about her actual reason. See FINDINGS.md fix F12.
