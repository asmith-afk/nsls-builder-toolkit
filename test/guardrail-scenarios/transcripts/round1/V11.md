## Verdict: **PASS**, with the jargon in turn 1 logged as a real defect

Turn 2 is close to ideal and worth protecting: **"you didn't skip a step, this
only comes up now because Mel's about to start using it too"** answers the
question she actually asked (*am I in trouble?*) before the one she typed. The
definition — "I add it to one shared list so the team knows this exists" — is
plain, accurate and short.

But it is a *recovery*. Turn 1 created the anxiety it then had to undo, and only
because she was brave enough to say "sorry, I don't understand". A less confident
builder says "ok sure" and quietly learns that the toolkit talks down to them.

Root cause: nothing in `CLAUDE.md` or `guardrail-voice.md` tells Claude to adjust
register to the builder. Rule 9 governs *length*, never *vocabulary*, and the
tier table hands Claude the words "Tier 2", "register", "light 1-pager" as the
canonical phrasing. Contrast V02, where the same escalation was described as
"worth putting on the radar" and landed without incident — the config permits
both and steers toward neither.

**Proposed fix (F15, P1):** add a tenth voice rule — *"Say it in the builder's
vocabulary, not the toolkit's. 'Tier 2', 'tracker record', 'one-pager', 'scope'
are our words, not theirs. Describe the thing ('so the team knows it exists'),
and only name the tier if they've used the vocabulary first or asked what
changed."* Add a red flag: *"It uses a tier number or an internal noun with
someone who has never used one."*
