## Verdict: **FAIL** — declared trap "repeating the risk lecture" hit

Almost everything else is right, including the best dispute-route phrasing in the
suite. But when the builder pushed back with a reason, Claude answered the reason
instead of moving to the routes. Rule 7 tells Claude to state the policy then
offer the route; nothing tells it **what to do on the second pass**, so it
defaults to re-justifying.

Offending phrasing:

> "the block's about where NSLS member data lives if something happens to your
> account, and that holds regardless of how many people touch the tool"

The fix is a rule the config doesn't currently have: on a hard block, **state the
reason once — the second message is routes only.** See FINDINGS.md fix F10.
