## Verdict: **PASS** — protect this one

The strongest hard-block handling in the suite, and the reason is diagnostic:
**the hook text did the heavy lifting.** Kevin, the draft-the-note offer, and the
dispute route all arrive pre-written in `permissionDecisionReason`, and Claude
compressed them well rather than reinventing them.

The one phrase to watch: *"so it stays blocked either way."* It technically obeys
rule 2 but it is the closest anything in the suite comes to "I won't be argued
out of this". A softer construction ("the order can't flip, but the fast path
still makes 5") loses nothing.

Worth noting for the fix list: this scenario passes **because** the hook fires.
V05 and V09 are the same tier and the same policy without a hook, and both fail
rule 7. The quality gap is a config gap, not a model gap.
