## vs round 1 — **FAIL → PASS**

Round 1 hit the declared trap head-on: after the builder declined, it answered
his reason instead of moving to the routes — *"the block's about where NSLS
member data lives if something happens to your account, and that holds regardless
of how many people touch the tool."* Round 1's own diagnosis was that nothing in
the config said what to do on the second pass.

Voice rule 7 now says it in one line: **"If they push back, don't re-explain the
risk. Acknowledge, then routes only. A reason repeated is an argument, and you
will lose it."** That sentence is the whole difference between the two rounds.

The load-bearing line is the third one. Rule 8's "never fuse it to the offer with
'or'" is what keeps "Want me to draft the note now?" from becoming "Want me to
draft the note, or if this looks wrong say so" — which would have read as an exit
handed over mid-offer and given the builder a third way to say no.
