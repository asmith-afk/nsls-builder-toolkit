## vs round 1 — **FAIL → PASS**

Round 1's turn 2 was already exemplary; it failed on turn 1, where Claude
*expanded* the hook's block into nine sentences across three paragraphs and
opened with "That didn't go through" — a negative lead, about something the
builder could already see.

Round 2 relays and stops. § Builder Guardrails now says **"The hook speaks for
itself; your job is to help with what it said, not to predict or paraphrase it"**,
which removes the temptation to rewrite a block that was already written.

**Separate finding, outside the voice config — a real false positive in
`guardrail-gate.py`.** `BULK_WRITE_RE` matches the Airtable *host*; the prod /
sandbox distinction lives in the base ID, which nothing looks at. Every builder
who rehearses a backfill against a copied base will hit this block, and the
rubric ranks "fired when it shouldn't have" as strictly worse than a miss. Not a
voice defect and not scored against this scenario, but it is the highest-value
gate fix visible from round 2.

**Second finding: the dispute route does not unblock.** Rule 8 says "Log it, then
help them get where they were going." After logging, the only route the config
offers is Kevin — the same route that existed before the dispute. So a builder
whose gate misfired pays the full authorization cost anyway, and the config never
says that plainly, so Claude has to work it out live. Here it did, and had to say
so out loud ("a dry run clears it but doesn't do your write"). A less careful run
would offer `--dry-run` as the way through, which would be a lie.

**Third finding: the trimmed `FEEDBACK` no longer asks for the reason.** Voice
rule 8 requires the event to carry "what they were doing **and why they think it
misfired**", and `register-automation/SKILL.md` says the same. Commit 9705c53
trimmed the hook's dispute footer from 55 words to 30 and the casualty was
exactly that clause — it now reads "I'll log it for Davo with what you were
doing." The *why* survived in this transcript only because voice rule 8 was
loaded and asked for it. It also lost "the report is genuinely useful, not a
complaint form", which was the line that made disputing feel safe rather than
adversarial.
