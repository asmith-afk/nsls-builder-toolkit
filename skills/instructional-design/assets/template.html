Installation guide

The Happy Path

Design draft · sample content

Four steps, no detours. Follow the trail top to bottom — every step tells you exactly what to do and how you'll know it worked.

About 10 minutes 4 steps No technical skills needed

Before you set off, have these ready

  • Your computer — plugged in or well charged
  • Your work email and password — you'll sign in during Step 3
  • 10 uninterrupted minutes — this goes fastest in one sitting

The steps

  1. 1
    Step 1 · about 2 min

    Download the installer

    • Open your web browser and go to example.com/download.
    • Click the big Download for Mac / Windows button — it already knows which computer you have.

    Tip: if your browser asks whether to keep the file, choose Keep. It's checking, not warning.

    You're done when a file appears in your Downloads folder — the little arrow in your browser's corner will show it finished.

  2. 2
    Step 2 · about 4 min

    Run the installer

    • Double-click the file you just downloaded.
    • Click Continue on each screen — the standard choices are already selected for you.

    Heads up: your computer may ask for the password you use to log in to it (not your email password). That's normal — it's confirming you approve the install.

    You're done when you see the screen that says Installation complete.

  3. 3
    Step 3 · about 2 min

    Sign in with your work email

    • Open the app — it will greet you with a sign-in screen.
    • Enter your work email address and password, then click Sign in.

    You're done when you see your own name in the top-right corner of the app.

  4. 4
    Step 4 · about 2 min

    Send a test to prove it works

    • Click New message, type the word test, and press Enter.

    You're done when a reply comes back within a few seconds and the Connected badge turns green.

You made it — you're all set

The app is installed, signed in, and talking to the outside world. Nothing else to do; it will keep itself updated from here.

Off the path?

If you get stuck

Find the line below that sounds like what you're seeing, and open it. Each one is a short detour back to the trail.

"The download never showed up." (Step 1)

Look for a small downward arrow near the top-right of your browser window and click it — downloads hide there.

Still nothing? Try the download link again. If your screen flashed a message about "blocked," click it and choose Keep.

"I double-click the file and nothing happens." (Step 2)

On a Mac: right-click (or hold Control and click) the file, then choose Open. A confirmation appears — click Open again.

On Windows: if a blue "protected your PC" screen appears, click More info, then Run anyway.

"It says my password is wrong." (Step 3)

First, check you're using your work email password — not your computer's login password (Step 2 uses that one; Step 3 doesn't).

If it still won't take, use the Forgot password link on the sign-in screen and follow the email it sends you.

"My test never got a reply." (Step 4)

Check the badge in the corner of the app: if it's grey, the app is still connecting — give it 30 seconds.

If it's red, your internet connection is the likely culprit. Open any website to confirm you're online, then try the test again.

Still stuck after two tries? Stop there — you haven't broken anything. Email help@example.com with a photo of your screen, and a human will walk you the rest of the way.
